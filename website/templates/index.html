Core Concept

The FactList — immutable foundation

The FactList is the single source of truth for every claim in the report. It is created once, validated by Gate 1a, and locked forever. No downstream agent may add, modify, or remove facts.

Final report claim ↓ (S2 claim cites source_fact_ids) FactList entry F012 verbatim_quote: "adjusted EPS increased 16 percent year-over-year" speaker: "Raj Subramaniam, President and CEO" ↓ (Gate 1a confirmed quote exists in transcript) Transcript source text: confirmed ✓
Three claim types

Every S2 claim must declare one

grounded — Directly supported by a FactList entry. Must cite source_fact_ids.

derived — Calculated or inferred from facts. Must cite source_fact_ids + derivation_note.

interpretive — Editorial judgment. Max ~20% of claims. Auto-passes Gate 2.

Seven fact types

Extracted by S1a across three passes

financial_metric  forward_guidance  management_statement  operational_metric  analyst_question  competitive_reference  prior_quarter_reference

Graceful degradation: Level 0 (all gates pass, full report) → Level 1 (Gate 2 rejects, retry) → Level 2 (partial output, omit section) → Level 3 (pipeline halt, no output). A FactList failure always triggers Level 3 — no partial output when the foundation is bad.

Cybernetic frameworks

Why cybernetics? Traditional software testing assumes a known correct output. AI generation systems produce outputs that are plausible but not deterministically correct — the failure mode is fluent hallucination, not a crash. Cybernetic frameworks are designed precisely for self-regulating systems that must manage variety, uncertainty, and observer bias. They are the right foundation for this problem class.
